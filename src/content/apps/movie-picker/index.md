Movie Picker is completly FREE and was built out of a personal need: to help movie lovers to find a Movie or TV Series to watch Fast!
You can discover top-rated movies and TV shows to watch on your favorite streaming platform, based on your mood or genres that matches your current vibe.

Development Process:

• Conducted experiments to determine Product-Market fit, including gathering user feedback,
implementing waitlist sign-ups, and executing beta testing.

• Developed the app from concept to launch with a Customer First approach, integrating user feedback
at every stage of the development process.

• Achieved a user base of 1,900 and reached a peak of 100 daily active users.

`Tech stack: Angular · TypeScript · Firebase`
