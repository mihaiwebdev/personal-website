A platform to facilitate collaboration between workers and clients for house chores.

It has distinct views for each user role (worker, client).
The client view streamlines a three-step process for service selection, scheduling, and worker selection.
The worker view enables profile creation, availability management and job tracking.

`Tech stack: React.js · Node.js · Express.js · MongoDB`
